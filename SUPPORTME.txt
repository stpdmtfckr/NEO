SUPPORT MY PROJECTS
Free software. Optional support. A heartfelt thank-you.
============================================================================

Hi! Thanks for using my software and taking an interest in my work.

I create desktop applications and tools, including NEO and my Qt Widgets
projects. This page is a way to support the person behind those projects -
not a store or a paywall.

My projects are free to use. Financial support is completely optional and
does not unlock features or change your access to public releases.

WHAT YOUR SUPPORT HELPS WITH
----------------------------

Even small applications take time to build and maintain. Your contribution
helps me dedicate more time to:

- Development: adding useful features and exploring new ideas.
- Maintenance: fixing bugs, improving reliability, and testing changes.
- User experience: refining interfaces, accessibility, and everyday
  workflows.
- Documentation: improving setup instructions, examples, and release notes.

There is no minimum expectation. Choose an amount that feels comfortable for
you - or simply keep enjoying the software.

SUPPORT ON BOOSTY

SUPPORT WITHOUT SPENDING MONEY
------------------------------

Thoughtful feedback is valuable, too. You can help by reporting a
reproducible bug, suggesting an improvement, improving documentation,
sharing a project with someone who might find it useful, or starring its
GitHub repository.

See FEEDBACK.txt for contact details and a ready-to-use bug report template.

A NOTE ON EXPECTATIONS
----------------------

Support is a voluntary contribution, not a purchase of custom development,
priority technical support, or a guaranteed release date. Feedback and
feature requests are welcome from everyone, whether or not they contribute
financially.

Thank you for helping me keep building useful software. Every kind of
support matters.
